description: Internal tools shouldn’t feel like they were built by different companies. To stop reinventing the wheel, our team at Walt Disney Animation Studios moved from a fragmented, app-by-app approach to a unified platform mindset sparked by just one designer and one engineer partnering to create a studio-wide asset. In this session, we’ll pull back the curtain on the creative tension between design and engineering, showing how debating and refining our choices allows us to distill complex studio workflows into seamless user experiences. We’ll look at how this single system serves as a universal blueprint, starting with our web library, adapting into desktop applications and third-party plugins, and scaling through a community contribution model where any team can help evolve it. Join us to see how streamlining our building blocks allows a lean team to scale 1-to-many, reclaiming the focus needed to build the impactful, magical features that actually move the needle for our productions.
